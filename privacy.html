Privacy Policy

Alba. Effective 1 September 2026.

Alba collects nothing. There is no account, no sign in, no server, no analytics and no third party code of any kind in the app. Everything you log stays on your iPhone.

What Alba stores, and where

Your medication, the doses you log, your weights, the side effects you record, your refill count and your settings are written to a database inside the app on your own device. This version of Alba does not sync to iCloud or anywhere else. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Apple Health

If you turn Apple Health on in Settings, Alba reads body mass from Health so your trend stays current, and writes back the weights you type so you do not enter them twice. Body mass is the only type Alba asks for, in either direction. Nothing read from Health is sent anywhere, and access is granted and revoked by you in the Health app, not by Alba.

Notifications

Alarms and reminders are scheduled by iOS on your device. They carry no health values. There is no push server.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Alba never sees your payment details, your name or your Apple Account. It receives only whether an entitlement is active.

What leaves your phone, and only when you ask

Two things, both started by you: an export file, and the prescriber PDF. Alba writes the file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes after that is your choice, and Alba is not involved.

Children

Alba is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Your rights

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing held about you to access, correct or delete. Your data is on your device and under your control: export it or delete the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it, and any change that affects what Alba collects will ship alongside an updated App Store privacy label.
