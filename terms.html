Alba. Effective 1 September 2026.

Alba is a logging tool, not medical advice. It does not diagnose, it does not treat, and it performs no dosage calculation of any kind. It records what you tell it and shows quotations from approved medication labels with links to the source. Every decision about your medication belongs to you and your prescriber.

Agreement

By using Alba you accept these terms. Alba is also covered by Apple's standard licence agreement for apps, the Apple Media Services Terms and Conditions (EULA), which applies in full.

What Alba is for

Keeping your own record: the dose your prescriber chose, when you took it, your weight over time, how you felt, and how many tablets are left. It presents the label's own administration instructions as a reference.

What Alba will not do

  • It never calculates, suggests, defaults or prefills a dose.
  • It never tells you which row of an escalation table applies to you.
  • It never tells you to start, stop, skip, raise or lower a medication.
  • It does not interpret symptoms or diagnose anything.

If you are unwell, or something about your medication worries you, contact your prescriber or pharmacist. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Accuracy

Label quotations are transcribed from approved sources listed on the Sources page and are provided as they are printed. Labels are revised by their manufacturers; the leaflet in your box and your pharmacist are authoritative over anything shown in the app.
