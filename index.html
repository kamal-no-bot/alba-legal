Common questions

Alba does not tell me what dose to take

That is deliberate and it will never change. Alba performs no dosage calculation of any kind. It records the dose you tell it your prescriber chose, and it shows the approved label's escalation table as a reference without ever indicating which row is yours. Any change to your dose is a conversation with your prescriber.

The morning alarm did not go off

Check that notifications are allowed for Alba in iOS Settings, and that the morning alarm is turned on in Alba's own Settings. On iOS versions before 26.1, Silent mode can silence it; Alba says so on the screen where you turn it on, because that is the operating system's decision and not something the app can override.

My weight is not coming in from Apple Health

Turn on Apple Health in Alba's Settings, then check Alba under Sources in the Health app. Health does not tell an app what it is allowed to read, so Alba cannot show you that state itself. Alba reads and writes body mass only, and nothing else.

Where is my data?

On your phone. Alba has no account and no server. This version does not sync anywhere. Use Export in Settings to take a copy whenever you like, free, always.

How do I cancel a subscription?

In the App Store, not in Alba: open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Alba's own Settings has a Manage subscription row that takes you straight there.

I paid before and Alba does not know

Settings, then Restore purchases. It works without buying anything and it is the right button after a reinstall or a new phone.

Alba is not medical advice

Alba is a logging tool. It does not diagnose, and it does not replace your prescriber or pharmacist. Talk to them about anything that worries you.
