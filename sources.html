Sources

Where every clinical statement in Alba comes from.

Alba quotes approved product labels and links to them. It does not paraphrase them, and where a label is silent Alba says to ask your prescriber rather than filling the gap.

Medication labels

Every label card in the app carries a tappable link to the label it quotes, on the screen making the claim. The labels are the FDA-approved prescribing information published on DailyMed, run by the U.S. National Library of Medicine.

  • DailyMed, U.S. National Library of Medicine

How the label content is used

  • Administration instructions (empty stomach, water allowance, the interval before food) are quoted as printed.
  • Escalation tables are shown as reference. Alba never indicates which row applies to a given person.
  • Adverse reaction information is shown only where it has been verified against the label PDF. Anything still unverified is withheld from the app entirely rather than shown with a caveat.

Clinical review

The label cards are read against their source before release. Where a review item is open, the card it belongs to does not ship.

Corrections

If you find a quotation that does not match its label, please write to engr.kamalnasir@gmail.com and it will be corrected.
